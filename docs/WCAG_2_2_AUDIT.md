# WCAG 2.2 Accessibility Audit

## Scope and claim

- **Product:** Regneflyt 2.57.2
- **Assessment date:** 2026-09-05
- **Primary target:** WCAG 2.2 Level AA across menu, timed and unlimited
  quizzes, results, settings, dialogs, notifications, and error routes.
- **AAA position:** Regneflyt implements additional applicable Level AAA
  criteria, but does not make an unqualified Level AAA claim. User-selected
  timed rounds intentionally end at zero and therefore do not satisfy SC 2.2.3
  No Timing. Unlimited rounds provide the no-time-limit process.
- **Locales:** Norwegian Bokmål, English, French, German, and Spanish.
- **Themes:** light, dark, forced colors, and reduced motion where applicable.

**Current disposition:** The implementation uses a focused answer field for
ordinary keyboard entry and includes a user control addressing the previously
identified SC 2.2.1 barrier. The evidence register below separates criterion outcome, evidence method, and
coverage. Open items remain explicitly non-conforming or undetermined; no test
command is represented as a conformance certificate.

Product invariants remain explicit: keyboard entry is active only while the
answer field has focus, persistent navigation stays isolated from quiz input,
focus is not moved into the number pad, answer cadence and adaptive scoring are unchanged,
and user-selected timed rounds still end at zero. The primary product target is
whole-product WCAG 2.2 Level AA; no unqualified Level AAA claim is made.

Automated checks are evidence for the criteria they exercise; they are not a
conformance certificate. Required semantic, linguistic, and assistive-technology evidence must be
recorded before publishing any broader conformance statement.

## Assessment vocabulary

These dimensions are independent. **Manual** is never a conformance outcome.

- **Outcome:** **Pass**, **Not met**, **Open**, or **N/A**.
- **Evidence method:** **Automated**, **Source**, **Semantic**, **Linguistic**,
  **AT**, or **Mixed**.
- **Coverage:** **Exhaustive** covers the complete declared inventory for that
  method; **Representative** covers selected routes, states, themes, locales,
  requirements, or processes. Exhaustive automation does not replace required
  semantic, linguistic, or AT judgment.

## Coverage inventories

### Canonical routes

`tests/e2e/appRoutes.ts` is the exhaustive registry of user-reachable routes used
by route-wide sweeps: menu, quiz, populated wrong-answer results, custom
configuration, settings, and not-found/error. New user-reachable routes must be
added there.

### Page-location classification for SC 2.4.5

| Route       | Classification                         | Multiple-ways disposition                                                                    |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `/`         | Independently locatable page           | Persistent global navigation and header home link provide multiple locating mechanisms.      |
| `/settings` | Independently locatable page           | Only persistent global navigation currently locates this page; a second mechanism is absent. |
| `/quiz`     | Step in the user-selected quiz process | Process-step exception. Persistent navigation remains available without stealing quiz input. |
| `/results`  | Result of the quiz process             | Process-result exception; persistent navigation remains available.                           |
| Error route | Contextual error response              | Reached from an invalid destination; recovery navigation is provided.                        |

### Canonical dynamic states

`tests/e2e/appStates.ts` separately inventories tagged transient/configured states
without multiplying every expensive route sweep: unlimited expanded navigation,
empty-answer validation, the complete-round dialog, and timed gameplay. Tags let
focused evidence select dialogs, validation, timing, or expanded navigation.
Results variants remain represented by the canonical populated wrong-answer route
and focused result-flow tests.

### Content and functionality scope

The assessed functionality includes quiz configuration, direct and on-screen
keyboard answer entry, timed and unlimited rounds, completion/quit dialogs,
results and answer-key presentation, progress-code save/load, destructive
progress deletion, accessibility preferences, locale/theme selection,
notifications, persistent navigation, offline/update messaging, and error
recovery. User-facing message catalogs for nb, en, fr, de, and es are the content
inventory; linguistic outcomes remain Open where native-language judgment is
required.
